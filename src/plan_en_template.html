01 / OBJECTIVE

Dose load with explicit rules

The aim is to apply the dose, measure and adjust cycle in rehabilitation with explicit rules, and to leave each step as public, reviewable evidence.

Method

What is done

Translate clinical trials into explicit progression rules: how much load to prescribe, what to measure after each session, and when to progress, hold or regress.

Use measures with known properties. For example, on the Arabic VISA-A the individual minimum detectable change is 28.4 points (Alghamdi 2026): a smaller change in one patient cannot be told apart from measurement error.

Deliverables

What is produced

A documented rules engine, with a citation for each rule. A reproducible analysis of the TEAch dataset. A prototype calculator. 2 to 3 anonymised clinical cases. All published openly on GitHub.

Why this project. Dosing load is one of the most frequent decisions in rehabilitation and one of the least documented. This plan turns it into explicit rules, each with its source, and into visible, verifiable work.
02 / MODULES

Three modules, one rules engine

The engine is shared: inputs (prescribed load, pain, 24 h response, questionnaire, strength), rules with their source, and an output (progress, hold or regress). The rules and thresholds change by region.

Module 1 · First

Achilles tendinopathy

This is the first module because it has load trials with explicit protocols, an open participant level dataset, and measures validated in Arabic with published measurement error.

Module 2

Hamstring injury

Local relevance: in the Saudi Professional League 2015 to 2016 (10 clubs, 300 players) hamstring strains were 11.8% of injuries (Muaidi 2019).

Rehabilitation rules: the lengthening protocol reduced return to training from 51 to 28 days on average in 75 Swedish footballers (Askling 2013). Prevention with the Nordic exercise: 3.8 versus 13.1 injuries per 100 player seasons in 942 players (Petersen 2011); 579 amateur players with 25 sessions in 13 weeks (van der Horst 2015).

Open data: physical load and hamstring injuries in elite football (Zenodo 10231709, CC BY 4.0), LaLiga injuries (figshare, CC0) and biomechanical deficits after injury (Ménard, figshare, CC BY 4.0). None is a rehabilitation progression dataset: there is no open rehabilitation data, so the rules will come from the trials and be checked against the project's own cases.

Module 3

Chronic low back pain

Everything collected on the chronic low back pain pages for Saudi Arabia (ES, AR): the 2026 Saudi guideline, international guidelines, dose evidence and gaps.

The rule set concept is already sketched there in seven rules, each with its source.

Two pieces of work planned: an analysis of the cognitive functional therapy trial dataset (CFT, Zenodo 6760585) and a study of adherence to the Saudi guideline among physiotherapists, continuing the line of Moslem 2022 and Alshehri 2020.

03 / DATA LIMITS

Why the three regions are not crossed with public data

The idea of relating the ankle, the hamstrings and the lumbar region in the same person is attractive, but it cannot be answered with open data today.

Limit 1

No open dataset found covers the three regions in the same people.

Limit 2

FinnGen publishes aggregate results; individual data are not openly available, so it does not allow following the same patient across regions.

Limit 3

Causal evidence for a kinetic chain between these regions is weak; a cross with aggregate data would only show correlations.

Where it does fit. As a doctoral question with a dedicated cohort in Riyadh, with the three regions measured in the same people, prospective follow up and ethics approval.
04 / DIFFERENCE

Hypothesis: load decisions with the evidence in view

A tool that works on a phone alone, in Arabic, English and Spanish, that decides to progress, hold or regress and cites the evidence for each decision.

05 / ROADMAP

About 12 weeks for the first module

The Achilles module first, complete and published. Then hamstrings and lumbar on the same engine.

Weeks 1 to 3 · Evidence review

Full text reading of Silbernagel, Beyer, LOADIT and the Arabic validations. A glossary of dose variables and of measures with their error.

Weeks 4 to 6 · Engine and TEAch

Rules for the Achilles module, each with its citation. Reproducible analysis of the TEAch dataset in a public notebook.

Weeks 7 to 9 · Calculator

A mobile web prototype in AR, EN and ES that returns progress, hold or regress with the rule and the source.

Weeks 10 to 12 · Cases and publication

2 to 3 anonymised clinical cases with consent. Open publication on GitHub.

After week 12. Module 2 (hamstrings) and module 3 (chronic low back pain), reusing the engine and the publication format.
06 / STUDY IN RIYADH

Requirements for a dedicated study in Riyadh

What is needed to move from clinical cases to a study with ethics approval, and what earlier Saudi studies show.

Requirements

Before working with patients

Interventions are carried out by professionals with a current licence from the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS).

A protocol approved by an ethics committee, informed consent and study registration before recruitment.

Ethics

Approval precedents

Ministry of Health committee: 20-194E (Alshabanat 2022) and 20-192E (Alshewaier 2024).

King Saud University committee: E-19-4315 (Alghamdi 2026).

Equipment

Proposed material

A phone with the calculator, the Arabic VISA-A, a 0 to 10 numeric pain scale, a step and a weight for heel raises, a metronome for tempo and, if possible, a handheld dynamometer or a load cell. An initial proposal, to be adjusted with the study team.

Related work

Earlier research in Saudi Arabia

Arabic validation of the VISA-A at King Saud University (Alghamdi 2026), the LOADIT feasibility trial on intensity and time under tension (Hasani 2021) and the Saudi low back pain guideline (Aldera 2026).

07 / RISKS

Risks and limitations

Data

There is no open data on rehabilitation progression; TEAch is not a dosing trial. The rules rest on small trials and on abstracts until the full texts are read.

Measurement

With an individual minimum detectable change of 28.4 points on the Arabic VISA-A, many week to week decisions cannot rest on that questionnaire alone.

Follow up

Losses of 30% and 38% in earlier Saudi studies, and few women recruited in at least one of them.

Regulation

A tool that recommends loads could be regulated as health software: confirm this with the competent authority before using it with patients.

Scope

Three modules are a lot for one person. Mitigation: finish and publish the Achilles module before opening the others.

Language and market

Arabic texts need native review. Competitors may already have similar features: their demos need review.
